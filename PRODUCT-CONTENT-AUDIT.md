# Product content audit

Reviewed 2026-10-15. All 55 products have a shared content record, used on Home, Products and the five category pages.

## Source checks

- Read the supplied front, left and right photographs for every product. Tonics and syrups were additionally inspected with rectified side-panel crops; this enlarges the existing pixels and cannot recover blurred text.
- Public catalogue: https://wasaylabs.com/wp-json/wc/store/v1/products?per_page=100 . It returned five products. All five short descriptions repeat Ginkgo Biloba copy, including unrelated Gaster, Loozen, I-V Leaf and Coughseel. Those mismatched descriptions were excluded.
- Google searches did not provide reliable Wasay-specific formulas. Other manufacturers’ similarly named traditional preparations were not substituted.
- Ingredient lists reproduce only readable names. No dosage, ingredient quantity, efficacy guarantee, certification or price was inferred. Partial lists are explicitly marked.
